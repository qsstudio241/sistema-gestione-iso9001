## Introduction

Qualification of welding procedures serves to demonstrate that production operations fully comply with the agreed welding procedure, including preliminary and subsequent treatment. Before a particular welding procedure is used in a production operation, the manufacturer should determine and document the suitability of the welding procedure specification (WPS) to produce a weld of the required quality. In this document, the term “welding procedure specification” comprises all the activities which influence the welding result, such as preparation, welding parameters, post treatment and reworking. All new welding procedure tests are intended to be carried out in accordance with this document from the date of its issue. Specification and qualification of welding procedures that were made in accordance with previous editions of this document can be used for any application for which the current edition is specified. ## 7.6 Re-testing

### 7.6.1 General

In the event of failure during NDT or destructive testing, 7.6.2 and 7.6.3 shall be followed to carry out retesting if proceeding further in accordance with the WPQR.

### 7.6.2 Related to NDT

If the test piece fails to comply with any of the requirements for NDT then:

a) an analysis shall be performed to determine the cause of the failure. If it is established that the cause

of failure is not procedure-related, for example due to parent material defects, equipment issues, insufficient welder’s or welding operator's skill and the examiner or examining body accepted the analysis result, then the destructive testing shall proceed in accordance with the WPQR. Details of the evidence shall be included in the report; or

b) one further test piece shall be welded, using the same parameters, and subjected to the same

examination. If this additional test piece does not comply with the requirements, the welding procedure test has failed.

### 7.6.3 Related to destructive testing

If the test piece fails to comply with any of the requirements for destructive testing required by Table 1 or Table 2, re-testing shall be made on two additional test specimens from the original test piece for each test specimen that failed if adequate material is available. The test specimens shall be taken as close as possible to the original specimen location. Each additional test specimen shall be subjected to the same tests as the initial test specimen that failed. If any of the additional test specimens do not comply with the requirements, the welding procedure test shall be considered failed. If there is insufficient material for re-testing, a new test piece shall be welded using the same parameters.

## 8.4 Common to all welding procedures

### 8.4.1 Welding process

The qualification is only valid for the welding process(es) used in the welding procedure test. For multi-process procedures, the welding procedure qualification test shall either be carried out with separate tests for each welding process or as a multi-process test.

Welding of a test in any one position in accordance with ISO 6947 (pipe or plate) qualifies for welding in all positions (pipe or plate) except for PG, PJ and PJ-L045 and SP (special test positions), where a separate welding procedure test is required.

The qualification shall be given for the type of current [alternating current (a.c.), direct current (d.c.), pulsed current] and polarity used in the welding procedure test.

### 8.4.7 Preheat temperature

When preheating is required, the lower limit of qualification shall be the nominal preheat temperature applied at the start of the welding procedure test. t The preheat temperature shall be determined for the thickest material in the joint. NOTE See ISO/TR 17671-4.

### 8.4.8 Interpass temperature

The upper limit of qualification shall be the highest interpass temperature reached in the welding procedure test.

### 8.4.9 Post-weld heat treatment or ageing

Post weld heat treatment, for example artificial ageing or natural ageing, shall be specified in the pWPS in accordance with ISO 2107. Addition or deletion of post-weld heat treatment or ageing is not permitted. The temperature range and the ageing conditions specified in the pWPS shall be the range of qualification.
